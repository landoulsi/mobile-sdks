### Minor Issues & Recommended Modifications

1. **Activity Lifecycle & Configuration Changes in Demo**:
   - In [`MainActivity.onCreate`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/app/src/main/java/com/landoulsi/payment/MainActivity.kt#L84-L87), [`CheckoutViewModel`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/checkout/CheckoutViewModel.kt#L28) is instantiated directly as a regular class rather than through an AndroidX ViewModel factory or retained state holder. A configuration change (such as screen rotation) will recreate the viewmodel and re-trigger availability checks, resetting any in-flight or completed payment state.

2. **Hardcoded String Literals in UI**:
   - While most strings are properly defined in [`strings.xml`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml), several user-facing strings in [`MainActivity.kt`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/app/src/main/java/com/landoulsi/payment/MainActivity.kt) remain hardcoded, such as `"Total Amount"` (line 290) and `"The payment sheet was dismissed. You can retry when ready."` (line 617). Moving these to [`strings.xml`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml) will improve localization readiness.

3. **Composable Coupling & Preview Strategy**:
   - [`CheckoutScreen`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/app/src/main/java/com/landoulsi/payment/MainActivity.kt#L146) is directly bound to [`CheckoutViewModel`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/checkout/CheckoutViewModel.kt#L28). Extracting a stateless composable `CheckoutContent(uiState, onGooglePayClick, ...)` would simplify previews and isolated Compose UI testing without needing a live ViewModel and coroutine scope.

4. **Thread-Safety of Availability Cache**:
   - In [`CheckoutViewModel`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/checkout/CheckoutViewModel.kt#L58), `availabilityCache` uses a standard `mutableMapOf`. If readiness checks or queries are accessed from multiple dispatchers simultaneously, consider using a thread-safe structure or synchronizing access.
