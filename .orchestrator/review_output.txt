### Minor Observations & Recommendations

1. **Billing Address Requirement Flag Merging**:
   - In [`GooglePayJsonFactory.createPaymentDataRequest`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/androidMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/googlepay/GooglePayJsonFactory.kt#L177), shipping address requirement is computed with `request.requireShipping || config.shippingAddressRequired`.
   - However, in [`GooglePayJsonFactory.createCardParameters`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/androidMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/googlepay/GooglePayJsonFactory.kt#L84), billing address requirement only inspects `config.billingAddressRequired`. If a caller supplies a [`PaymentRequest`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/model/PaymentRequest.kt#L27) with `requireBillingAddress = true` while `config.billingAddressRequired` is `false`, the request-level flag will not be reflected in the payload. Consider passing `request.requireBillingAddress || config.billingAddressRequired` when constructing the card parameters.

2. **Configurability of `existingPaymentMethodRequired`**:
   - [`GooglePayJsonFactory.createIsReadyToPayRequest`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/androidMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/googlepay/GooglePayJsonFactory.kt#L117-L120) supports `existingPaymentMethodRequired`, but [`GooglePayConfig`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/model/GooglePayConfig.kt#L153-L175) does not expose this setting. Adding an optional property to [`GooglePayConfig`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/model/GooglePayConfig.kt#L153) would allow apps to differentiate between whether Google Pay is supported vs. whether the user has a card on file ready to pay.

3. **`totalPriceStatus` Dynamic Support**:
   - In [`GooglePayJsonFactory.createPaymentDataRequest`](file:///Users/ahmed/Work/payment-sdk/mobile/shared/src/androidMain/kotlin/com/landoulsi/payment/shared/googlepay/GooglePayJsonFactory.kt#L152), `totalPriceStatus` is hardcoded to `"FINAL"`. This is standard for immediate checkouts, but if estimated cart totals or authorization holds are needed later, exposing a price status enum would provide added flexibility.
